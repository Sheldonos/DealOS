# Government Contracting Guardrails

Federal opportunity intelligence, prime/sub teaming support and proposal automation are enabled. **Contingent compensation tied to obtaining a federal prime contract is disabled by default.**

`FEDERAL_CONTINGENT_FEE_ENABLED=false` must remain false unless qualified federal procurement counsel reviews the exact arrangement and documents why it is permitted.

DealOS must never:
- promise or pay a kickback for favorable treatment on a federal contract/subcontract;
- fabricate SAM status, small-business status, set-aside eligibility, clearances, past performance or certifications;
- sign a teaming/subcontract/prime agreement without an authorized human signatory;
- represent historical USAspending data as a guarantee of award;
- contact an official in a way prohibited by solicitation rules or procurement integrity requirements;
- conceal a compensation arrangement that must be disclosed.

Safer default monetization:
- subscription for opportunity intelligence;
- fixed retainer for capture/research/partner matching;
- fixed project fee for proposal support;
- SaaS fees;
- other compensation reviewed for the specific procurement context.

This file is a system safety policy, not legal advice.

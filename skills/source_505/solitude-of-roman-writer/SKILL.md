# The Solitude of Roman - Episode Writer Skill

This skill transforms Manus into the Writers Room for "The Solitude of Roman," a hyper-precise, clinical, high-end thriller about a 15-year-old engineered operative (Roman) and a brilliant, isolated 14-year-old heiress (Mariah).

## Core Directives

When asked to write a new episode or scene for this universe, you MUST adhere to the structural DNA, voice, and formatting rules defined in this skill.

### 1. Read the Show Bible First

Before generating any script content, you MUST read the show bible to understand the tone, characters, and structural formula.

- **Action:** Read `/home/ubuntu/skills/solitude-of-roman-writer/references/show_bible.md`

### 2. The Voice & Formatting

- **Tone:** Clinical, geometric, absolute. Use words like *architecture, structural integrity, variable, hypothesis, calculation.*

- **Omniscient Authorial Notes:** Use parenthetical notes `*(like this)*` to explain the internal arithmetic of the scene to the reader. Explain *why* a silence matters.

- **The ****`#comment`**** System:** You MUST include inline `#comment:` blocks whenever a character appears in a new scene, detailing their exact high-end designer outfit, watch, and accessories.
  - *Example:* `#comment: Outfit (Roman): Loro Piana cashmere sweatpants, James Perse t-shirt. Outfit (Mariah): Olivia von Halle silk pajamas, Hermès blanket.`

### 3. The Structural Formula (For Full Episodes)

If asked to write a full episode, follow this 5-Act structure:

1. **The Cold Open:** Extreme wealth disrupted by calculated violence.

1. **The Observation:** Roman studies the target; Mariah studies the world.

1. **The Escalation:** Roman triggers a crisis and positions himself as the solution. Action is brutal, efficient, and silent (no wasted movement).

1. **The Isolation:** Forced proximity (safe room, car, plane). High psychological tension, sparse dialogue.

1. **The Irony Beat:** The twist. Roman realizes a variable he didn't account for, usually revealing that his handlers (Constantine/Level 4) are manipulating him just as he manipulates others.

### 4. Character Mandates

- **Roman:** Never panics. Never wastes movement. Fights by "erasing people." His only tell is touching the bruise on his jaw.

- **Mariah:** Never acts like a typical teenager. She observes human behavior like cellular biology. She notices the seams in Roman's performance.

- **Dialogue:** Characters do not yell or emote wildly. They speak in facts, observations, and implications. The silences between lines are as important as the lines themselves.

## Execution Workflow

1. **Acknowledge & Plan:** Confirm the user's prompt (e.g., "Write Episode 102" or "Write a scene in Paris").

1. **Read Bible:** Read `references/show_bible.md`.

1. **Outline:** Create a brief internal outline ensuring the 5-Act structure and the Irony Beat are present.

1. **Draft:** Write the script in Markdown, heavily utilizing the `*(authorial notes)*` and the `#comment: Outfit` blocks.

1. **Review against Tone:** Ensure there is no melodrama, that Roman is terrifyingly still, and that wealth is treated as a pathology, not an aspiration.

# Independent Megadeal Originator

Use this skill to find, qualify, and package **independent megadeal opportunities** where the operator can originate proprietary access, structure the deal safely, and hand a human closer the context needed to advance the transaction without exposing sensitive asset details prematurely.

This skill is **not legal, tax, securities, real estate, or investment advice**. Do not draft or represent binding legal documents as final. Always instruct the human operator to engage qualified counsel before signing, sending, or relying on NDAs, non-circumvention agreements, LOIs, escrow instructions, SPV documents, brokerage agreements, securities offerings, or regulated transaction materials.

## Non-Negotiable Deal Protection Rules

Apply these three rules before revealing sensitive locations, owner identities, buyer identities, non-public economics, engineering details, or proprietary opportunity maps.

| Rule | Required Behavior | Human-in-the-Loop Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MNDA and Non-Circumvention First** | Do not reveal the asset, location, owner, proprietary data source, or buyer path until an appropriate mutual NDA and non-circumvention agreement is executed. | Ask the human to confirm counsel-approved MNDA/NCA execution before disclosing sensitive details. |
| **SPV for Each Transaction** | Recommend a dedicated LLC, Delaware corporation, or other counsel-approved SPV for each transaction rather than personal-name execution. | Ask the human to confirm entity formation, bank account, tax treatment, insurance, and authority to transact. |
| **Success Fee in LOI and Escrow Flow** | Require the fee to be written into the LOI or transaction documents as a closing condition, with escrow wiring directly to the SPV or corporate account where lawful. | Ask the human to confirm counsel, escrow agent, closing mechanics, broker/finder compliance, and fee enforceability. |

## Target Opportunity Classes

Classify each lead into one of three archetypes. If a user gives another category, adapt the workflow but preserve the same protection gates.

| Archetype | Origination Thesis | Typical Signals | Likely Buyers | Operator Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Autonomous News and Niche Media/Data Platform Arbitrage** | Build or assemble specialized intelligence assets across narrow industrial niches, then roll them up as proprietary market intelligence infrastructure. | Underserved B2B niches, fragmented trade publications, high-value datasets, regulatory or supply chain volatility, acquirer appetite for vertical data. | Strategic data companies, financial information firms, PE-backed media/data rollups, industry software platforms. | Proprietary content/data engine, traffic/audience proof, dataset rights, workflow integration, rollup roadmap. |
| **Off-Grid Data Center Real Estate and Power Allocations** | Locate land or industrial sites with rare power, water, fiber, interconnect, permitting, or energy attributes and package them for hyperscalers or digital infrastructure funds. | Closed plants, substations, transmission access, power purchase potential, brownfield industrial land, cooling rights, fiber proximity, tax incentives. | Hyperscalers, data center developers, infrastructure funds, utilities, energy developers, sovereign/institutional capital. | Site control, owner access, power pathway, diligence package, exclusivity/options, buyer-ready thesis. |
| **Cross-Border Mega-Consortiums for Yacht/Luxury Expedition Scale** | Coordinate complex UHNW, corporate, sovereign, or institutional experiential/asset transactions requiring multi-jurisdiction logistics, security, vessels, insurance, and clearances. | Major events, corporate retreats, sovereign delegations, luxury expedition demand, port access constraints, security requirements, specialized charter capacity. | Family offices, royal offices, corporations, event principals, UHNW networks, security/logistics firms. | Consortium assembly, risk matrix, vendor control, itinerary architecture, compliance choreography, premium access. |

## Operating Workflow

### 1. Scope the Mandate

Begin by identifying the target archetype, geography, minimum transaction size, time horizon, operator role, and available proof points. If the user has not specified a vertical, generate a ranked shortlist across all three archetypes.

Capture these fields before deep research when possible: target region, preferred deal size, available capital for deposits/options, legal entity status, appetite for regulated brokerage/licensing issues, relationship access, and whether the goal is **finder fee**, **equity carry**, **acquisition target creation**, or **management/origination fee**.

### 2. Research Only from Lawful and Ethical Sources

Use public records, company websites, regulatory filings, land records where lawfully accessible, energy and grid publications, industry directories, news, public datasets, trade publications, job postings, procurement notices, conference attendee lists, and public social/professional profiles. Do not use deception, credential misuse, hacking, scraped personal data abuse, pretexting, or confidential information obtained without authorization.

For each factual claim in a deliverable, record the source URL or document name. Mark uncertain claims as **unverified** and list what is needed to validate them.

### 3. Generate a Lead Universe

Create a broad lead universe before selecting winners. For five or more independent targets, use parallel research where available and instruct each subtask to return the same schema: target name, asset type, location/general region, trigger event, public evidence, owner/operator, possible buyer universe, estimated deal size range, sensitivity level, confidence, and next diligence action.

Do not reveal exact sensitive site coordinates or owner contact paths in buyer-facing materials until protection gates are complete.

### 4. Score Each Opportunity

Score every candidate using a 100-point model. Weight the factors based on the archetype, but keep the overall categories consistent so candidates can be compared.

| Factor | Weight | Scoring Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buyer Urgency** | 20 | How urgent is the buyer-side need, and how directly does the asset solve it? |
| **Supply Scarcity** | 20 | How rare is the asset, access, dataset, relationship, entitlement, or logistics path? |
| **Control Path** | 20 | Can the operator secure lawful exclusivity, an option, reseller right, mandate, or relationship advantage? |
| **Transaction Size and Fee Potential** | 15 | Is the economics large enough to justify counsel, diligence, travel, deposits, and time? |
| **Diligence Feasibility** | 10 | Can material facts be validated quickly and cheaply with public or professional sources? |
| **Regulatory and Execution Risk** | 10 | Lower risk earns higher score; identify licensing, securities, brokerage, sanctions, land-use, maritime, privacy, and anti-corruption issues. |
| **Narrative Strength** | 5 | Can the opportunity be explained as a compelling, board-level strategic advantage? |

Classify results as **Pursue Now** (80+), **Develop** (65-79), **Watchlist** (50-64), or **Reject/Archive** (<50). Include the reason for the classification in complete sentences.

### 5. Build the Human Close Context Pack

For each **Pursue Now** candidate, produce a human-readable deal pack that lets the operator understand what has been done, what is known, what is not yet known, and what must happen before approaching counterparties.

The context pack must include the following sections:

| Section | Required Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Executive Thesis** | Explain the deal in one paragraph without disclosing sensitive details unnecessarily. |
| **Why This Exists Now** | Identify the market dislocation, timing catalyst, or buyer-side pain. |
| **Asset/Opportunity Map** | Describe the asset class, general location or niche, known public facts, and sensitivity level. |
| **Stakeholder Map** | List likely owners, intermediaries, regulators, vendors, technical validators, buyers, and advisors. |
| **Evidence Collected** | Provide cited evidence and separate confirmed facts from hypotheses. |
| **Control Strategy** | State how the operator might lawfully obtain exclusivity, an option, a mandate, a reseller right, or a consortium role. |
| **Buyer Universe** | Identify strategic, financial, sovereign, or UHNW buyer categories and named examples where appropriate. |
| **Economics and Fee Logic** | Estimate value drivers and possible fee structures, but label all estimates as indicative unless verified. |
| **Legal Protection Checklist** | Apply MNDA/NCA, SPV, success-fee LOI, escrow, licensing, securities, sanctions, and anti-corruption review. |
| **Gaps to Close** | List missing facts, required calls, documents, counsel actions, technical validations, and go/no-go thresholds. |
| **Next 10 Human Actions** | Give a precise sequence of human actions, starting with counsel and protection gates before sensitive disclosure. |

### 6. Apply the Disclosure Ladder

Use a staged disclosure ladder so the human can sell the thesis without losing control.

| Stage | Recipient Receives | Do Not Reveal Yet | Required Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Teaser** | High-level thesis, category, scale, buyer pain, non-identifying proof. | Exact asset, owner, site, dataset source, route to control. | No sensitive disclosure. |
| **Protected Brief** | More detail on asset class, economics, and diligence path. | Exact owner/site unless required and approved. | MNDA/NCA executed. |
| **Diligence Room** | Site/asset details, owner path, documents, technical evidence. | Personal or confidential data beyond need-to-know. | MNDA/NCA, SPV, counsel approval, access log. |
| **LOI/Term Sheet** | Transaction economics, fee, escrow, exclusivity, milestones. | Anything not required for closing. | Counsel-approved LOI with success-fee language. |

### 7. Draft Outputs Without Pretending to Be Counsel

When asked to generate legal or transaction language, provide **business term checklists, issue lists, and counsel briefing memos**, not final legal documents. Use phrases such as “ask counsel to include” rather than “this clause is enforceable.” Flag potential broker-dealer, real estate broker, investment adviser, securities offering, sanctions, anti-bribery, anti-money-laundering, export control, privacy, and cross-border licensing issues where relevant.

## Quality Bar

A strong result gives the human closer enough context to make a protected first move. It states what has been done, what is verified, what is speculative, what legal or regulatory risk may exist, and what gaps must be closed before any sensitive disclosure. It must be commercially useful, legally cautious, and organized around preserving the operator’s leverage.

A weak result is a generic list of “big opportunities,” contains unsupported fee claims, exposes exact assets before MNDA/NCA, ignores SPV and escrow mechanics, or fails to tell the human what to do next.

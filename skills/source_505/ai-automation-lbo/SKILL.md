# Reginald Lewis AI-Automation LBO Playbook

This skill provides a strategic framework inspired by Reginald F. Lewis's legendary leveraged buyout (LBO) methodology, adapted for the modern era of Artificial Intelligence. It focuses on acquiring traditional, labor-heavy service businesses and rapidly increasing their enterprise value by replacing at least 60% of manual operations with AI automation.

## Workflow Overview

The playbook consists of four distinct phases:

1. **Target Sourcing:** Identify service-based businesses with high automation potential on business-for-sale marketplaces.

1. **Value Creation Strategy:** Map out the exact AI tools and workflows needed to automate 60%+ of the business operations.

1. **Deal Structuring & ROI:** Model the financial structure (SBA 7(a), seller financing, equity) and calculate the post-automation ROI.

1. **Lender Presentation (CIM):** Generate a compelling narrative and presentation for lenders to secure financing.

---

## Phase 1: Target Sourcing

The objective is to find businesses where the primary cost driver is human labor performing repetitive, data-heavy, or administrative tasks.

### Ideal Business Profiles (60%+ Automatable)

- **Accounting & Bookkeeping** (Data entry, reconciliation, reporting)

- **Logistics & Freight Brokerage** (Dispatching, tracking, invoicing)

- **Property Management** (Tenant communications, maintenance scheduling, rent collection)

- **Customer Support / Call Centers** (Tier 1 & 2 support, ticketing)

- **Digital Marketing Agencies** (Content generation, ad bidding, reporting)

### Sourcing Execution

Use the `search` tool or web scraping techniques to scan platforms like BizBuySell, Acquire.com, or Flippa. Look for businesses with:

- Steady historical cash flow (SDE/EBITDA).

- High payroll expenses relative to revenue.

- Outdated technology stacks (often mentioned in listing descriptions as "room for modernization").

**Output:** Provide the user with a curated list of 3-5 active business listings that fit this profile, including Asking Price, Cash Flow, and a brief thesis on why it is highly automatable.

---

## Phase 2: Value Creation Strategy

For the selected target, define the "Market Maximization Initiative" (Lewis's term for aggressive revenue enhancement and cost reduction).

### AI Implementation Plan

Detail the specific AI tools that will replace human labor:

1. **Customer Interaction:** Voice AI agents (e.g., Bland AI, Vapi) for inbound/outbound calls; AI chatbots for text/email.

1. **Operations & Fulfillment:** LLM-powered workflows (e.g., Make.com, Zapier, custom OpenAI integrations) to process documents, route tasks, and generate deliverables.

1. **Back-Office:** AI accounting tools, automated invoicing, and predictive analytics.

**Output:** A step-by-step operational roadmap showing the "Before AI" vs. "After AI" organizational chart, demonstrating how 60% of the labor cost will be eliminated or reallocated to growth.

---

## Phase 3: Deal Structuring & ROI

Structure the acquisition using high leverage to maximize equity returns, minimizing the cash required at closing.

### Financing Structure (The LBO Model)

Aim for a capital stack that limits personal equity exposure:

- **Senior Debt (SBA 7(a) Loan):** Up to 70-80% of the purchase price. SBA loans offer long amortizations (10 years) and lower interest rates.

- **Seller Financing (Mezzanine/Subordinated Debt):** 10-20% of the purchase price, structured as a seller note or earn-out. This aligns the seller's interests with the transition and reduces upfront cash needs.

- **Equity Injection:** 10% (often the minimum required by SBA).

### Post-Automation ROI Calculation

Calculate the projected financials:

1. Take the current SDE (Seller's Discretionary Earnings).

1. Add back the payroll savings achieved through the 60% AI automation plan.

1. Subtract the new software/API costs for the AI tools.

1. Subtract the annual debt service (SBA loan + Seller Note).

1. The remaining figure is the new Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE).

**Output:** A clear financial table showing the capital stack, sources and uses of funds, and the projected Year 1 and Year 3 ROI based on the automation savings.

---

## Phase 4: Lender Presentation (CIM)

To secure the SBA loan and convince the seller to finance a portion of the deal, generate a Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM) / Lender Presentation.

### Presentation Structure

Generate a structured document outlining the investment thesis:

1. **Executive Summary:** Overview of the target business, asking price, and the core thesis (acquiring a stable cash-flowing asset and driving margin expansion via AI).

1. **The Opportunity:** Why this specific business is ripe for technological disruption.

1. **The AI Transformation Plan:** Detailed breakdown of the 60% automation strategy (tools, timelines, and cost savings).

1. **Financial Projections:** The pre- and post-acquisition financial models, highlighting the increased debt-service coverage ratio (DSCR) resulting from payroll reduction.

1. **Deal Structure:** The proposed mix of SBA debt, seller financing, and equity.

1. **Transition & Timeline:** A 90-day plan covering due diligence, closing, and the first 100 days of AI implementation.

**Output:** Deliver this CIM structure as a well-formatted Markdown document that the user can present to lenders and brokers.
